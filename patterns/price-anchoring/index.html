Price Anchoring & Fee Architecture

The first number a user sees becomes the anchor against which all subsequent prices are judged. Strikethrough pricing, fee decomposition, surge pricing, and tiered plan comparisons all manipulate the reference point. The question isn't 'is this worth $X?' but 'is this cheap compared to the anchor?'

Psychological Principle
Anchoring Effect (Tversky & Kahneman, 1974) — initial exposure to a number influences subsequent numerical judgments, even when the anchor is arbitrary
