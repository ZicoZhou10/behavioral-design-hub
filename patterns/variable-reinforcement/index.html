Variable Ratio Reinforcement

The most addictive schedule in behavioral psychology: unpredictable rewards at unpredictable intervals. Pull-to-refresh, content feeds, match notifications, and loot boxes all use the same mechanism as slot machines — the uncertainty of the next reward keeps users pulling the lever.

Psychological Principle
Operant Conditioning (Skinner, 1957) — variable ratio schedules produce the highest, most consistent response rates and are most resistant to extinction
