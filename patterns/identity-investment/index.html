Identity Investment & Self-Expression

Products that become part of how users see themselves create the deepest retention. Taste profiles, curated boards, streaming histories, professional identities, and customized spaces all store self-concept data. Leaving isn't just losing a tool — it's losing a piece of identity.

Psychological Principle
Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan, 2000) — autonomy, competence, and relatedness needs drive intrinsic motivation; identity investment satisfies all three
