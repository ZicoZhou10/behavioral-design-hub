Progressive Commitment & Escalation

Start with a tiny ask, escalate gradually. Free trials, setup wizards, profile completeness meters, and progressive disclosure all exploit the consistency principle — once users take a small step, they're more likely to take bigger ones to remain consistent with their self-image as someone who 'uses this product.'

Psychological Principle
Consistency Principle (Cialdini, 1984) + Foot-in-the-Door Technique — small initial commitments increase compliance with larger subsequent requests
