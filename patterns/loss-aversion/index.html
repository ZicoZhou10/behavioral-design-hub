Loss Aversion & Sunk Cost

Products exploit our tendency to weigh losses more heavily than equivalent gains. Streak mechanics, sunk cost membership models, and switching cost barriers all leverage the endowment effect — once users have invested time, money, or social capital, the pain of losing it outweighs the rational cost-benefit analysis.

Psychological Principle
Prospect Theory (Kahneman & Tversky, 1979) — losses are felt ~2x more intensely than equivalent gains
