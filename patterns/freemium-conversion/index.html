Freemium Conversion Mechanics

The art of giving enough value for free to create dependency, then gating the features users now 'need' behind a paywall. Successful freemium conversion creates a moment where users feel the free tier is limiting them — not where they feel manipulated.

Psychological Principle
Endowment Effect + Loss Aversion — once users have experienced premium features (via trial), returning to free feels like a loss
