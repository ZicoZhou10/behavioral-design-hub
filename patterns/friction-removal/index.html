Friction Removal & Simplification

Every step, click, or decision point is a potential exit. Products that remove friction win — but there's a dark side: removing friction from harmful actions (impulsive trades, excessive spending) can be more manipulative than helpful. The question is: friction removal for whose benefit?

Psychological Principle
Cognitive Load Theory (Sweller, 1988) — reducing extraneous cognitive load frees mental resources for the target behavior
