Ecosystem Lock-In & Network Effects

The strongest competitive moat in digital products: when the product becomes more valuable as more people use it, and leaving means losing access to the entire ecosystem. App marketplaces, two-sided platforms, and creator economies all create switching costs that grow with usage.

Psychological Principle
Network Effects (Metcalfe's Law) + Endowment Effect — the value of the network grows quadratically with participants; users overvalue what they already possess
