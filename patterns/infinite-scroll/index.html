Infinite Scroll & Stopping Cue Removal

Removing natural end-points from content consumption. Infinite scroll, autoplay, and bottomless feeds eliminate the decision points where users might choose to stop. Without a page break, chapter end, or 'next' button, users must actively decide to leave — and most don't.

Psychological Principle
Bottomless Bowl Effect (Wansink, 2005) — removing consumption cues leads to 73% more consumption without increased satisfaction
