Gamification & Achievement Systems

Points, badges, leaderboards, levels, and progress bars import game mechanics into non-game contexts. When aligned with genuine value (learning progress, fitness goals), gamification motivates. When misaligned (profile completeness meters, arbitrary points), it manufactures engagement without delivering value.

Psychological Principle
Operant Conditioning + Flow Theory (Csikszentmihalyi, 1990) — optimal challenge-skill balance + immediate feedback creates intrinsically motivating experiences
