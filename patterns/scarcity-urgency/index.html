Scarcity & Urgency Signals

Artificial time pressure and limited availability trigger fear-based decision-making. Countdown timers, 'only X left' badges, ephemeral content, and flash sales bypass rational evaluation by activating the amygdala's threat response. Real scarcity informs; manufactured scarcity manipulates.

Psychological Principle
Prospect Theory + Reactance Theory — perceived loss of availability increases perceived value (Cialdini, 2021)
