Social Proof & Validation

We look to others' behavior when uncertain about our own choices. Reviews, ratings, 'X people are viewing this', endorsement notifications, and social validation loops all exploit our tendency to follow the crowd — especially powerful when the 'crowd' is curated or manufactured by the platform.

Psychological Principle
Social Proof (Cialdini, 1984) — in ambiguous situations, we assume others' actions reflect correct behavior
