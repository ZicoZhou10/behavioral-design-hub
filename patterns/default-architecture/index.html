Default & Choice Architecture

The most powerful behavioral design technique: what happens when users do nothing. Autoplay, opt-out renewals, pre-selected options, and algorithmic feeds all exploit the status quo bias — most users never change defaults. The designer who controls the default controls the behavior.

Psychological Principle
Nudge Theory (Thaler & Sunstein, 2008) — defaults are the most effective nudge because they exploit status quo bias and require zero cognitive effort
