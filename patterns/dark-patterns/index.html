Dark Patterns & Deceptive Design

Design choices that benefit the business at the user's expense. Difficult cancellation flows, hidden fees, confusing opt-outs, and risk-normalizing interfaces cross the line from persuasion to manipulation. Increasingly regulated — EU, FTC, and California have all enacted dark pattern legislation.

Psychological Principle
Cognitive Overload Exploitation — deliberately increasing decision complexity to guide users toward the business-preferred outcome
